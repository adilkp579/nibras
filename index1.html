Our Kitchen in Poonoor

At Nibras, our chefs are more than just cooks — they are the keepers of a culinary legacy built on authentic Malabar spices, time-honoured techniques, and a deep love for Kerala's food traditions. Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the fingerprints of hands that have spent years perfecting the art of slow cooking, and the heart of a team that treats every guest like family. Our Poonoor kitchen is led by culinary masters — biriyani specialists, dessert artisans and grill masters — all united by one mission: to serve you the finest flavours of Kerala with warmth, care, and consistency. Explore the chefs and the team behind Nibras below, and discover the people who make it a place worth returning to.

Our Story

From a Small Kitchen to Poonoor's Favourite

A journey of passion, tradition and the warmth of Kerala hospitality — told in four chapters.

01

A Humble Beginning

What began as a small kitchen in the heart of Poonoor, with a single stove, a handful of recipes, and an unwavering dream.

02

The Signature Taste

Our legendary Manthi and Biriyani were born — crafted with authentic Malabar spices, slow-cooked tradition, and a secret passed down through generations.

03

A Growing Family

Families, travellers and food lovers made Nibras their own. Every plate served became a memory shared, every guest a friend.

04

Poonoor's Favourite

Today, we stand proud as one of Poonoor's most beloved dining destinations — still cooking with the same love, the same spices, and the same heart.
